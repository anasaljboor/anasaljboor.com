How a project works

The same four steps for a one-week fix or a three-month build.

  1. Free 20-minute call

    You tell me what you need and by when. I ask about users, budget and what already exists. Pick a time.

  2. Written proposal

    Within a few days you get the scope, a fixed price, the timeline and what I need from you.

  3. Build with weekly demos

    You see working software every week on a test link, and can change course early.

  4. Launch and handover

    I deploy to your accounts and write down how it runs, so you or another developer can take it from there.

Questions clients ask

Do you work with clients outside Jordan?

Yes. I'm based in Amman and work remotely with clients anywhere, most often in Jordan, the Gulf and Europe, in Arabic, English or German. Jordan shares a time zone with Saudi Arabia, is one hour behind the UAE and one to two hours ahead of Germany, so the working day overlaps with all three.

How much does a website or web app cost?

It depends on the scope. After a short call I send a written proposal with a fixed price and timeline, so you know the full cost before any work starts.

Can you take over an existing project?

Yes. I start with a short review of the code, hosting and deployment, tell you what I would fix first, and then take over builds, deploys and fixes.

Who owns the code and the servers?

You do. The code lives in your GitHub account and the hosting runs in your cloud account. I document how to deploy it, so another developer can pick it up.

Are you open to full-time roles?

Yes, for full-stack and DevOps roles, on-site in Amman or remote. Download my CV.
